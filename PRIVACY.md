# Privacy Policy

**Good Energy | Scan the Can** (Android app `com.drinkgoodenergy.scan`)

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This app doesn't collect, sell or share your personal data. Here's what it does with information.

## What stays on your phone

Your scans, balance, streak, flavor pick, claimed can codes and your 18+ answer are saved on your device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app deletes them. "Reset demo" clears your scans.

## Your wallet

When you connect a Solana wallet through Mobile Wallet Adapter, the app saves your public wallet address and the wallet's session token on your device so you stay connected. Your private keys stay in your wallet app. The app only asks your wallet to sign claim messages. It never sends a transaction.

## No servers, accounts or tracking

The app has no backend, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads and no third-party trackers.

## Sharing and links

If you tap "Share my cut", the app hands the text to your phone's share menu, and you choose where it goes. Links to outside websites open in your browser, and those sites have their own policies.

## Age

Scan payouts are 18+. The app isn't meant for anyone under 18.

## Changes

If anything here changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
